# Alert & Triage Architecture

## Overview
Phase 4 introduces a human-in-the-loop alert and triage system. It translates deterministic engineering risk signals (from Phase 3D fusion and 3E temporal analysis) into actionable workflows for counsellors.

## Core Principles
1. **No Autonomous Clinical Decisions**: The AI does not diagnose, prescribe, or execute medical interventions. It merely flags engineering anomalies for human review.
2. **Deterministic Rules**: Alerts are generated by explicit, auditable engineering rules (e.g. Risk > 75), never by opaque LLM decisions.
3. **Deduplication**: Alerts are deduplicated by `case_id` + `alert_type` + `is_resolved=False` to prevent spamming the counsellor with duplicate active alerts.
4. **Auditability**: Counsellor actions (acknowledge, resolve, case notes) are recorded.

## Alert Priorities
*   **CRITICAL**: Engineering risk score >= 75. Recommended immediate review according to organizational protocol.
*   **HIGH**: Engineering risk score 49–74 or persistently worsening trajectory.
*   **MEDIUM**: Worsening trajectory or conflicting multimodal signals.
*   **LOW**: Insufficient data quality.

## Alert Types
*   `RISK_THRESHOLD`: Triggered by absolute baseline severity.
*   `WORSENING_TRAJECTORY`: Triggered by a recent negative trend.
*   `PERSISTENT_WORSENING`: Triggered by a long-term negative trend.
*   `SIGNAL_CONFLICT`: Modalities disagree significantly.
*   `INSUFFICIENT_DATA`: Not enough data to assess properly.

## Alert Lifecycle (State Machine)
1. **OPEN** (`is_read = False`, `is_resolved = False`)
2. **ACKNOWLEDGED** (`is_read = True`, `is_resolved = False`) - The counsellor has seen the alert.
3. **RESOLVED** (`is_resolved = True`, `resolved_by = CounsellorID`) - The counsellor has addressed the alert and provided intervention/notes.
4. **DISMISSED** - Same mechanical state as resolved, used when the alert required no action.

## Counsellor Workflow
1. A check-in triggers an alert.
2. The counsellor views their dashboard and sees the active alert.
3. The counsellor views the AI's deterministic explanation.
4. The counsellor reviews the case details and recent history.
5. The counsellor takes action (e.g. contacts the user).
6. The counsellor adds a Case Note documenting the action.
7. The counsellor marks the alert as Resolved.

## Notification Abstraction
For Phase 4, the `AlertService` acts as the trigger point for future notifications. Real SMS or emergency responder integration is strictly prohibited in this phase.
